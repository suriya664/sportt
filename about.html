Who We Are

More Than Just A Team

Founded in 2010, UNITY SC has grown from a local neighborhood initiative to a regional powerhouse, serving over 500 athletes across multiple age groups and skill levels.

Our comprehensive approach combines athletic training, academic support, and personal development to create well-rounded individuals who excel both on and off the field. We believe in nurturing talent while building character through the power of sports.

Our Mission

To foster athletic excellence and community spirit through sportsmanship, discipline, and teamwork.

Our Vision

To be the leading sports academy in the region, recognized for developing world-class talent and outstanding citizens.

Our History

From humble beginnings to becoming a regional sports powerhouse, our journey has been marked by dedication, growth, and countless achievements.

2010

Club founded by local legends John Doe and Mike Smith with a single football squad.

2015

Opened the Youth Academy, expanding to over 200 registered young athletes.

2020

Won the Regional Super League for the first time, securing our place in history.

2026

Launched the new high-performance training center and Unity Sports Club digital platform.
